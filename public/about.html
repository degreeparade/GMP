About IPO Pulse

A clear view of Indian IPO activity and grey market signals.

What we track

IPO Pulse brings together Indian IPO names, issue price information, bidding dates, grey market premiums, indicative listing prices, and simple sentiment ratings in one dashboard.

How the data works

Market data is collected from publicly available IPO GMP sources and refreshed by the project’s scheduled data update. Values can change during the day and may be unavailable for new issues.

Use the information responsibly

GMP figures are unofficial market estimates, not exchange prices or investment recommendations. Review official offer documents and consult a qualified financial adviser before making investment decisions.
